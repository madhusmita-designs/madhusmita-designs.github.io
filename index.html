About Me

Design that earns its place on the page

I'm a Senior Graphic Designer with 3+ years of experience creating visual identities, campaigns, packaging and digital experiences that help ideas communicate with clarity and character.

My work spans Healthcare, Education, Real Estate, Events, Hospitality and Coaching, giving me the ability to adapt my visual approach while keeping every design purposeful and distinctive.

I enjoy working across the full creative process — from developing concepts and visual directions to refining layouts, building brand systems and creating campaign-ready assets. I also explore AI-assisted workflows to expand creative possibilities while keeping design decisions intentional and human.
